Delivered a <a href='https://un-task-team-for-scanner-data.github.io/production-systems-survey/pdf/Survey-of-CPI-Production-Systems.pdf'>report</a> and <a href='https://un-task-team-for-scanner-data.github.io/production-systems-survey/presentation/presentation.html#/title-slide'>presentation</a> summarizing the results of a Survey of National Statistics Offices (NSOs) for 70 countries on CPI system modernization on behalf of the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe's (UNECE) <a href='https://unstats.un.org/bigdata/'>Committee of Experts on Big Data and Data Science for Official Statistics</a>. This work was presented at the UNECE's <a href='https://unece.org/info/Statistics/events/394156'>2025 Meeting of the Group of Experts on Consumer Price Indices</a>.
